# media/

Renders from the game (Godot Movie Maker, Adam's band room, Fletcher/Terence) for the post and demo.

- `video/showcase.mp4`: 50 s showcase: the band playing Whiplash, Terence conducting, the drummer's
  view with the drum chart on the stand, the fist cutoff, a (clean) tirade, the count back in.
- `video/fletcher_cutoff_clip.mp4`: 15 s drummer's-view cutoff with a clean line.
- `stills/`: promo stills (band sections, Terence's moves at their key frames, the stand menu and
  drum chart); `stills/captions.md` has a caption per image, `stills/contact_sheet.jpg` shows all.

Music in the videos is our own render of a Songsterr arrangement (personal use); fine for a
hackathon demo, check before reusing elsewhere.
