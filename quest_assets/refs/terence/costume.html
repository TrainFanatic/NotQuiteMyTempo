    Fletcher's (J.K. Simmons) costume with hat from Damien Chazelle's jazz-based drama Whiplash. Demanding perfectionist music instructor Fletcher wore his costume during a particularly grueling late-night rehearsal with drummer Andrew (Miles Teller), after which he gave the jazz ensemble instructions for their upcoming showcase.

    This costume consists of a beige plaid Panama-style hat; a black, relaxed-fit polyester and cotton blend blazer with a black cotton T-shirt and matching pants; a black striped knit scarf; and a pair of black rubber-soled leather loafers. The costume and its components are in good overall condition.
