# Animations

Generated by `gen_animations_md.py` from the exported glbs. Everything runs at 24 fps and 120 BPM (12 frames per beat). To follow a song, scale playback speed by `song_bpm / 120`. Frame numbers below are at 24 fps.

## Conventions

- **Characters** face -Y in Blender (+Z in glTF). Place them at their room marker with yaw = marker yaw + 180 degrees.
- **Band instruments** ride the extra root bone `Instrument`; the trombone slide rides `InstrumentSlide`, which moves only along the instrument's local -Y. In Godot, attach the instrument glb under a `BoneAttachment3D` on that bone with a +90 degree rotation about X (not identity: imported joints keep Blender's bone axes). See `StateMachineBuilder.ATTACH_OFFSET` in the Godot project.
- **Godot naming**: Godot's importer strips a `_loop` suffix (`play_loop` arrives as `play`). The Godot project's post-import script restores the name and sets loop modes from the table below.
- **Terence's props** ride `PropR` / `PropL` the same way (same +90 degree X offset). At frame 0 the prop bone sits where the prop should already be.
- **Default targets** (Terence's local frame at spot_terence): the player's head at (3.2, -3.75, 1.15). Close-up moves (t3_*, t5_rhythm_slaps, t5_cowbell) expect Terence 0.70 m in front of the player, facing them, with the head at (0.0, -0.7, 1.15).
- **Faces**: Terence's face is a photo texture with no shape keys, so there is no facial animation.
