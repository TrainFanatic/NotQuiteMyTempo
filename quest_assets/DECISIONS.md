# Decisions log (overnight run)

Each entry: what was undecided, what was chosen, and where to change it.

- **Overnight run executed inside the Claude Code session** instead of `run_overnight.sh`: the CLI login in Terminal failed with 401 on every retry, so nothing ran. Same rules, same files.
- **A1, 16 band members, not 17.** The OVERNIGHT name ranges (2+2+1+4+4+1+1+1) add up to 16. The 17th player is the drummer, i.e. the real person. Change: add an entry to `MEMBERS` in `build_band.py`.
- **A1 triangle target.** Members land at 1,916 to 2,076 triangles (target 2,000). Finger hands (needed for SkeletonProfileHumanoid finger bones) cost about 260 triangles per body. The hard cap is `TRI_TARGET = 2300` in `build_band.py`.
- **A1 base bodies.** These are three male bodies, `slim` 1.75 m, `average` 1.79 m and `broad` 1.83 m, matching the mostly male student band in the film. The table is `BASES` in `build_band.py`.
- **A1 faces** are painted procedurally (eyes, brows, nose, mouth, facial hair, glasses) into a 128 px texture and projected onto the front of the head, PS2 style. Only Terence uses a photo. The code is `paint_face()` in `build_band.py`.
- **A1 hair** is a mesh shell over the scalp (`ps2.hair_cap`) for short, curly, long, beanie and cap styles, and texture only for buzz and bald. The cap adds a box brim.
- **A1 sleeves and tucking.** Plaid, shirt and hoodie tops get long sleeves. Tees, stripes and polos are short-sleeved. Plaid, hoodie, tee and stripes are untucked.
- **A2 instrument frames.** Each instrument is modelled "as played", with the player facing -Y. Winds have their origin at the mouthpiece tip, the guitar at its body centre, the bass at the endpin's floor contact, and the stand on the floor. Grips are `grip_l`/`grip_r` empties parented to the root (the trombone's `grip_r` is on the slide). See the docstring of `build_instruments.py`.
- **A2 trombone slide.** `trombone_<n>_slide` is a child object that moves only along local -Y, from 0 (first position) to 0.58 m (seventh). It also stores this as custom properties. Change `SLIDE_TRAVEL` to adjust.
- **A2 variants.** The two alto saxes, two tenors and four each of trumpets and trombones share their shapes per type and differ by lacquer finish (gold, dark gold, pale gold, silver). The table is `FINISH` in `build_instruments.py`.
- **A2 music stand.** The reader stands on its -Y side, the same as the room's stands. The open folder and sheet music face the reader.
- **A3 props.** Props Terence handles have grip_l/grip_r empties. The throwable chair faces -Y (character convention) with its grips on the backrest top. The cowbell stick is a separate child object (`cowbell_stick`). Each of the 4 loose pages is its own object with both sides textured, so Godot's backface culling doesn't hide them. The knockable stand is split into base, pole and desk children, with a `pivot_tip` empty on the base edge away from the reader.
- **A4 seating.** The saxes are seated, from seat_sax_1 to seat_sax_5: tenor 2, alto 2, alto 1 (lead, centre), tenor 1, baritone. Brass seats are numbered to match their parts. Change `SEATING` in `build_review_scene.py`.
- **A4 placement rule.** Characters face -Y in Blender and markers face +Y, so in Godot a character's yaw is the marker's yaw + 180 degrees.
- **A4 bassist** stands behind the bass (as in the film) instead of sitting. The preview pose is in `build_review_scene.py`; the real one comes from B1.
- **A4 preview poses** (seated, instruments at rest) are look-dev only and are not exported. `ps2.seated_ops()` in lib_ps2 is shared with the B1 animations.
- **B1 instrument attachment.** Every band rig has an extra root bone `Instrument` (trombones also get `InstrumentSlide`, a child moving only along the instrument's local -Y). The animations move it. In Godot, put the instrument glb under a `BoneAttachment3D` on `Instrument` with an identity transform, and the trombone slide under one on `InstrumentSlide`. This avoids depending on hand-bone axes, which differ between Blender and Godot. Verified in Godot 4.5.1 later: the attachment needs a +90 degree X rotation, not identity (imported joints keep Blender's bone axes). The tests in the Godot project check it.
- **B1 posing.** Arms use two-bone IK (`ps2.ik_arm`) onto the instrument's `grip_l`/`grip_r`, read from `instruments/<kind>_1.glb`, so the hands follow the instrument. Placements are in `PLAY`/`REST` in `band_anims.py`.
- **B1 timing.** 24 fps, 120 BPM = 12 frames per beat. `play_loop` is 4 beats (2.0 s), with keys every half beat and Cycles modifiers. Godot can scale speed by tempo/120.
- **B1 per-player variation.** `look_at_drummer` is computed from each member's seat to `spot_drummer` in the room (head and upper-chest yaw, clamped to ±110°). The bassist stands (as in the film), the pianist plays the room's grand piano (no Instrument motion), and the guitarist strums.
- **B1 export.** glTF export leaks actions between rigs that share bone names, so each member is exported and has its actions deleted before the next member is built. The line-up blend therefore holds no actions; rebuild with `build_band.py` to regenerate them.
- **B2 Terence v002.** v001 is untouched. `terence_v002` rebuilds the same body and photo head on the lib humanoid (finger bones, 1,980 tris) and adds root bones `PropR`/`PropL`. A move that uses a prop keys the prop bone to the prop's transform: at frame 0 it sits where the prop is expected (chair beside him, drum on the floor, cymbal at his right, pages and stand in front, cowbell in his left hand). Godot attaches the prop glb under a BoneAttachment3D on that bone with an identity transform. For `t5_cowbell`, the stick (`cowbell_stick`) must be reparented onto `PropR`.
- **B2 target frames.** Moves are authored with Terence's node at `spot_terence` facing the band. Aimed moves use the player's head at spot_drummer, 1.15 m (local (3.2, -3.75, 1.15)). The close-up moves (t3_*, t5_rhythm_slaps, t5_cowbell) assume Godot first places Terence 0.70 m in front of the player, facing them (`NEAR_DIST`). `t3_circle_kit` circles at 1.3 m. `t0_chair_rest` sits him on `stool_terence`. `t4_charge` covers 1.6 m toward the drummer, and `t4_storm_out` walks to the main door (the motion is on the Hips bone, usable as a root-motion track).
- **B2 t5_cowbell interpretation.** He holds the cowbell beside the player's head and strikes it on every beat (5 hits) instead of throwing it.
- **B2 knockable stand.** `t4_sweep_stand` knocks over `props/music_stand_knockable.glb`. Godot should hide the room's static `stand_terence` while this move plays.
- **B2 no facial animation.** Terence's face is a photo texture with no shape keys, so faces are skipped as the work order allows. Expression changes would need extra mouth/brow textures (PS2-style texture swaps).
- **Polish room v004.** `build_room.py` now writes `room/room_<VERSION>.glb`. `room.glb` stays the v003 export because the overnight rule says never overwrite. The other scripts still read `room_v003.blend`, since the markers are identical in both.
- **Phase C in the existing game project.** It's built in `~/not-quite-my-tempo` (a fresh Godot 4.5 Mobile project), using Godot 4.5.1 from ~/Downloads. Assets are copied in with `tools/sync_assets.sh`. A post-import script restores `play_loop` (Godot strips `_loop`) and sets loop modes.
- **Terence v003** (`build_terence_v003.py`). v001 and v002 are untouched. He is 1.93 m tall, with shoulders x1.12, chest x1.08, hands x1.12 and head x1.05 over the shared humanoid before the overall scale. The photo face UVs are computed in the unscaled head frame. Bone names and the PropR/PropL convention are unchanged. Every action is keyed on every frame from monotone cubic pose curves ("hit" keys arrive at speed), with a breathing and weight-sway layer. The build samples every frame of every action and stops if a hand, forearm or prop goes deeper than 1.5 cm into the torso, neck, head or leg capsules. Results are in `terence/terence_v003_clearance.json`.
- **v003 chair throw.** `t5_chair_throw` uses `props/chair_folded.glb` (folded flat, grips on the top bar). It is slung flat like a discus: swung out at arm's length to his right, coiled, then whipped round and released at frame 28. It spins 2 full turns clockwise about its short axis on the way to DRUMMER_HEAD (frame 44), then drops and skids. A backhand frisbee throw was tried first and dropped, because the trailing slab passes through his chest.
- **v003 `walk`** is a utility loop (`utility: true` in the moves json, not one of the 45 move IDs). It walks in place, 1 s per cycle, with the hips over the origin. Move the node forward at `speed_mps` (1.44 m/s) so the feet stay planted.
- **v003 staging changes needed for reach.** Some v002 contacts were silently out of reach (IK clamps), and the build now checks this. To fix them, `t3_whisper` and `t3_fake_warmth` step in and bend from the hips, `t5_rhythm_slaps` lunge in, `t5_drum_throw` stoops deep to a drum placed 0.60 m in front of him, and `t5_cowbell` holds the bell 0.40 m to the player's side.
- **Band v004 clearance check.** `build_band.py` now measures every action of every member with `band_clearance.py` (sampled every 2 frames): instrument points against convex hulls of the body parts (thighs, shins, torso, head, upper arms, forearms), deformed body vertices inside the instrument mesh, hands and forearms against the body, and the body against the room's chair (seat and backrest), the piano bench and keyboard, or the bassist's stool. The worst values go to `logs/band_clearance_v004.txt`, and the build fails if a check passes its limit in `band_clearance.LIMITS`. Fingers that wrap a tube a little are expected, so hands get looser limits than instruments.
- **Band v004 seat pose.** Players sit on the front half of the chair (hip joint 5 cm forward of the seat centre, clear of the backrest), with the pelvis rolled back 20 degrees and the heels lifted 4 cm. The hip height is fitted per member so nothing sinks more than 4 mm into the cushion. This lives in `Player.seat_ops` and `fit_seat` in `band_anims.py`, not in `ps2.seated_ops`, which is unchanged for the review scene. The pianist sits on the room's 0.51 m bench the same way.
- **Band v004 instrument placement.** Saxes rest on their (implied) neck strap, hanging from the upper chest just below the playing position, so raise is a short lift. The trumpet lies on the right thigh. The trombone stands on its slide at the left side. The guitar sits on the right thigh against the belly. Placements can be relative to the mouth, the upper chest, the right thigh or the character origin (`PLAY`/`REST` in `band_anims.py`).
